I'm an Electrical Engineering student with a deep interest in Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and understanding how things work.

I enjoy exploring ideas across Computer Science, Mathematics, and
Engineering, from algorithms and intelligent systems to the dynamics of physical systems and the mathematics behind them.

Outside academics, I'm usually programming, experimenting with AI
models, reading about something that caught my attention, or working on a project. I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood, whether that's a machine learning model, a computer system, or a system described by a set of equations.

My interests currently lie around Machine learning, Computer systems, Signal processing, Control theory, and Mathematical modelling.

I like moving between these areas, picking up unfamiliar concepts and finding ways to put them into practice.

This website is where I keep some of the things I've built, explored,
and found interesting along the way. I'm always happy to connect with people who enjoy discussing technology, engineering, or an interesting idea.

Feel free to reach out!
